NAIROBI ITALIAN ICE & EATS
DIGITAL ECOSYSTEM DEVELOPMENT BRIEF
Project Objective

Develop a modern, scalable, enterprise-grade digital ecosystem that transforms Nairobi Italian Ice & Eats from a traditional dessert outlet into a technology-enabled lifestyle brand.

The platform must support:

Multi-channel sales
Customer loyalty
Inventory management
Delivery management
Business intelligence
Corporate catering
Subscription commerce
Future franchise expansion

The architecture should be capable of supporting multiple branches without requiring major redevelopment.
